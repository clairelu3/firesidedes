# Storytime movie: style spec + beat template

Derived from 15 watercolor references (motion/refs). No reference artwork is copied; motifs are original.

**Palette** (k-means over all refs): peach #cf937c · sky #6a9cb8 · sand #e2bc9c · deep blue #386b86 · rose #c5535c · mustard #d2a22c · cocoa #814f3c · leaf #6f9a5b (added). Ink #242119. Paper #f6f2e9 with grain.
**Line**: thin wobbly ink outlines slightly off-register from the fill; 8 fps boil (3 jitter variants).
**Fill**: watercolor pools with noisy edges, granulation, darker rim.
**Motifs**: round characters with dot eyes, highlights, blush, small smile, dangling legs (jellyfish/duck refs). Each character keeps one look across the whole movie (brother = blue with cap, grandma = peach with bun, mom = rose, cousins = green trio).
**Type**: Chalkboard SE (hand-lettered), STHeiti for Chinese.

## Beats (per storybook)
| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Title card, washes, sun + first character pop in | soft bells + pad |
| each card 4.8 s | lines reveal one by one, native line below; characters bob | rising pentatonic pluck per line |
| last 0.45 s of each card | camera pans to the next card; staying characters hop across, leaving ones drift off, new ones arrive | swish |
| last 3.2 s | "Told by Grandma · fireside", grandma + heart | resolving arpeggio + bell, fade |

The viewer pauses at each card's `rest_time` so Next/Back step through scenes of one continuous film.
